<h1>About</h1>

There is a steady stream of fantastic events for digital professionals forever occurring across the globe. However, it isn't always easy to keep track of them all, and even with the best of intentions some are sure to slip through the cracks. TechConf.Directory's goal is to aid your discovery of such events.

The directory came about because I found it difficult to not only find conferences but also to easily find information about many conferences in one place. It is invaluable to be able to access details on a huge number of conferences and then both filter and sort through them. It allows planning of trips to attend multiple conferences consecutively and the opportunity to stumble across events you might otherwise remain unaware of.

With a [fully open API](/docs) providing comprehensive data for your needs, this is not just a site but also an up-to-date source of information for whatever you may choose to build. If you do find yourself making something cool, be sure to forward it through!

TechConf.Directory will always remain free and independent. Unlike some other directories, discoverability and promotion cannot be bought. The site is run and maintained for the benefit of all, out of pocket. If you'd like to say thanks, you can financially support Declan Chidlow [via their website](https://vale.rocks/support).

For some further details about the project, you can refer to my [portfolio write-up](https://vale.rocks/portfolio/techconf-directory).
